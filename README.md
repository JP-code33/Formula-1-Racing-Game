# Formula-1-Racing-Game

I have made a Formula 1 style 2D racing game built with **Python** and **Pygame**. You race against a computer controlled car, progress through easy levels to difficult ones. You can pick the car you want and pick the opponent car and try to beat the computer!

# Features
1. Formula 1 style racing game
2. Computer opponent which chooses the best racing line!
3. Choose your own Formula 1 car and your opponent's car
4. Formula 1 style starting lights
5. Pause Menu
6. Settings Menu
7. How to Play Menu
8. Turn On/Off sound effects
9. Volume slider
10. Hover animations
